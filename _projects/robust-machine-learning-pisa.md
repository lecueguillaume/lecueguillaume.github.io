I organized, with Matthieu Lerasle and Adrien Saumard, a session on "robust machine learning" at [CMStatistics 2018](http://cmstatistics.org/CMStatistics2018/fullprogramme.php), which took place in Pisa, 14-16 December 2018 (Saturday 15.12.2018, 18:10 - 19:25, room Aula C). Speakers were:

1. I. Giulini, Dimension-free PAC-Bayesian bounds for vectors and matrices
2. S. Minsker, Median-of-means-type estimators and rates of convergence in normal approximation
3. P. Thompson, On stochastic approximation under heavier tails
